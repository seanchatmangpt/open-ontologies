# Nations Ledger — Bible O*

## Doctrine
Nations are external witnesses, not judges.
The Nations Ledger records what external systems, observers, and parties have witnessed about the wall-building work.
Nations do not govern the work. They observe it.

## NationsGate Refusal
There is no "NationsGate" in the Nehemiah structure.
Nations are not an admissibility boundary. They are a witness field.
Any reference to a "NationsGate" is an anti-pattern. See nehemiah-52.ttl bos:NationsGate (deprecated).

## bos:NationsLedger
The NationsLedger records external witness signals:
- What external parties have observed
- Whether the observation is validating or adversarial
- What the witness signal implies for the work

## bos:NationsLedgerRecord fields
- bos:witnessSource — who observed
- bos:witnessSignal — what was observed
- bos:witnessClassification — VALIDATING / ADVERSARIAL / NEUTRAL
- bos:hasReceipt — what evidence was emitted

## Operating Rule
Nations signal in; they do not decide.
The Inspection Gate decides, using Nations signals as evidence.

## Example
A published academic citation of this ontology package is a NationsLedgerRecord: VALIDATING.
A hostile review is a NationsLedgerRecord: ADVERSARIAL — routed to the Mockers model.
