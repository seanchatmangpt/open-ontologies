# Usury Ledger: Internal Extraction Audit

Usury is not external opposition. It is internal extraction. This is what makes it more
dangerous than mockery: the mocker stands outside the gate; the usurer stands inside the
swarm.

## 1. What Usury Is in This Context

Usury = the extraction of resources from builders by members of the same building community,
using the builders' need as leverage. In Nehemiah 5, the mechanism is:

1. Builders are working on the wall without stopping.
2. Builders are running out of food for their households.
3. Builders are mortgaging fields, vineyards, and houses to buy grain.
4. Nobles and rulers are lending money and grain — then demanding repayment at interest.
5. Builders are selling their children into servitude to cover debts to their own brothers.

The wall-building mission is consuming the builders' resources. The internal brokers are
capturing those resources instead of returning them to mission capacity.

## 2. Audit Trigger Conditions

The Usury Ledger records an audit when any of the following conditions exist:

- Builders are mortgaging capital assets to fund basic operations
- Interest is being charged internally between members of the building community
- Builder households are being reduced in capacity by debt servitude
- Swarm resources are flowing out of the mission to internal brokers

These are not soft signals. When Nehemiah hears the outcry in Neh.5.1-6, the audit is
immediate: "I was very angry when I heard their outcry and these words. I took counsel
with myself, and I brought charges against the nobles and the officials."

## 3. Audit Resolution Protocol

The audit record must include the demanded restoration, not merely the finding:

Neh.5.11 demands:
- Return their fields, vineyards, olive orchards, and houses
- Return the hundredth of money, grain, wine, and oil you have been exacting from them

Neh.5.12-13 records the oath:
- Nobles swore before the priests to do as demanded
- Nehemiah shook out his garment and said: "So may God shake out every man from his house
  and from his labor who does not keep this promise"
- The congregation said "Amen" and praised the Lord

The audit is not closed until the demanded restoration is confirmed. An audit finding
without a confirmed resolution is PARTIAL, not ALIVE.
