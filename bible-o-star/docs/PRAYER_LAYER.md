# Prayer Layer — Bible O*

## Doctrine
Prayers submit the work to God. They are upward appeals, not system calls.
The Prayer Layer is not a gate. It is a submission channel.

## bos:PrayerLayer
The PrayerLayer carries appeals upward from:
- Builders who encounter adversarial feedback
- Gates that have reached a decision boundary
- The Inspection Gate before issuing a verdict
- PropheticProclamation emissions

## bos:Prayer fields
- bos:prayerText — the appeal content
- bos:prayerSource — who submitted
- bos:targetGate — which gate this prayer accompanies
- bos:hasReceipt — evidence of submission

## Operating Rule
Every ALIVE claim should have a corresponding Prayer: acknowledgment that the verdict is not self-sufficient.
